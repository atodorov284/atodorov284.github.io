    • Many people have heard of Adam, but are not aware that Adam is not guaranteed to converge to a stationary point. That is, one can construct a convex function for which Adam does not converge, while simple algorithms like SGD have convergence guarantees. Adam is so-called adaptive because it adapts the learning rate for each parameter based on estimates of the moments of the gradients, and is built on top of earlier versions such as RMSProp and AdaGrad. A convergence proof of AdaGrad and a short overview of adaptive optimizers is available in this document.
