Research & Background

Research Statement

My research ideas focus on continual and reinforcement learning, with an emphasis on understanding how agents can learn efficiently over time while retaining past knowledge. I try to approach these problems from both theoretical and practical perspectives, often integrating ideas from optimization theory, probabilistic modeling, and control systems. Ideally (and also what I am currently working towards), is that I would like to see my research contributing to the development of agents that are able to learn in a lifelong manner, adapting to new tasks and environments while retaining, and possibly improving, previously acquired skills.
