Research Interests

My research interests lie primarily in reinforcement learning and continual learning, with a broader curiosity for multitask learning, meta-learning, and deep learning in general. I often integrate ideas from (Bayesian) probabilistic modeling, systems and control, and convex optimization, drawing on my background in mathematics to approach learning problems from both theoretical and practical perspectives.
Recently, I've been enjoying a lot of JAX, which has (somewhat unexpectedly) taken over PyTorch's spot in my daily workflow, especially for reinforcement learning. Right now, I am mostly working on a few projects related to continual and reinforcement learning with sprinkled-in theoretical optimization, and will be looking for a PhD position, ideally to start in summer 2027.

  • Reinforcement learning
  • Continual and meta learning
  • Probabilistic modelling
  • Optimization for adaptive systems
  • Bayesian statistics
