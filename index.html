About

I am a Master's student in Artificial Intelligence and Applied Mathematics at the University of Groningen, where I also completed my Bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence with the highest distinction Summa Cum Laude and graduated first in my cohort. I am motivated by the growing gap between what modern learning systems can do and our conceptual and mathematical understanding of why they work. I am interested in questions that try to close this gap and where understanding the structure of learning systems can make them more efficient, reliable, and amenable to analysis.

Beyond research and work, I try to maintain a balanced and healthy lifestyle, both physically and mentally. My cat, Beans (who you might spot on my GitHub profile picture), i.e., my unofficial co-researcher and mental health supervisor, plays an important role in keeping me grounded and reminding me to take breaks by ensuring I cannot hyperfocus on one thing for too long.

Research Interests

My interests lie primarily in continual learning, optimization, reinforcement learning and control. More broadly, I am interested in how learning systems can continue adapting as their experience changes while operating under constraints on memory and computation, preserving what remains useful, and allowing us to understand when and why their behavior may become unreliable. In terms of methodology, I like drawing on ideas from convex and online optimization and systems and control theory. In the past few years, I have been enjoying JAX more and more, especially for reinforcement learning.

I am currently working on projects in continual and reinforcement learning, and am looking for a PhD position, ideally starting spring, summer, or autumn 2027.
